What moved it down

  • K365 gained 4.4B of power in the last 7 days against our 2.9B (+53%). Prep points come from exactly this kind of growth: building, training and research.
  • K365 had 179 Town Centre upgrades in 7 days to our 117, and more governors active over 30 days (1,418 vs 1,060).
  • Prep is a whole-kingdom effort. K365 has 1,371 governors on the map to our 1,191.

What holds it up

  • We have never lost a prep phase (16-0). K365's only loss (14-1) was its first KvK.
  • Our top 6 alliances were at least as active as theirs in the 15 Sep snapshot (97% vs 95% active within 7 days).

K365 favouredEvenK203 favoured

Previous estimate: 45–55%.

In our favour

  • Our top 5 players carry 1,792M real power to K365's 1,573M (+14%). Our #5 player is stronger than their #2.
  • ORM, PRO and BR4 all have a median Town Centre of 70. K365's matching alliances sit at 70, 67 and 66.
  • By total power including troops, ORM (43.0B) and PRO (40.8B) out-size LTR and AOS.

Against us

  • K365 leads real power at every one of the top 5 alliance ranks: 61.3B against our 56.1B. Their hero and gear depth is spread wider.
  • Our biggest gap is alliance #4: SCC has 32.4B to SRT's 27.2B.
  • K365's top 4 alliances logged in more often day to day (94% active within 24h vs 88% for ours, 15 Sep snapshot).

Net: the centre stays at even. Our top-end edge and K365's depth roughly cancel. Prep and castle results line up only about 31% of the time across kingdoms, so the prep result says little about castle day.

Castle-day plan for K203

  1. Fight where we are strongest

    Build the main rallies around ORM, PRO and BR4. This TC70 core holds our top-end advantage. Keep their strongest marches on the castle and avoid spending them on secondary targets.

  2. Cover the SCC gap

    SCC is K365's #4 alliance and out-sizes SRT by 5.2B. Do not leave SRT to face SCC alone: pair it with reinforcements from HnG and BR1, and plan which core rally answers SCC when it commits.

  3. Lock in attendance before Friday

    In the 15 Sep snapshot, 24h activity in PRO, BR4 and SRT was 84%, 90% and 82%, against 94–99% for LTR, AOS and ORG. Get attendance confirmed in each top-6 alliance by Fri 9 Oct, with named backups for rally leaders.

  4. Give lower-TC members support jobs

    HnG and BR1 have median Town Centre 55 and 50. Use them to reinforce garrisons and fill rallies rather than in open-field fights against K365's TC65–70 alliances.

  5. Expect a stronger tail than their activity suggests

    WYW and SDH (K365 #5 and #6) were their least active alliances, but they are close to HnG and BR1 in power. Treat them as equals, not easy points.

  6. Don't read the castle result from prep

    K365 is likely to push hard in prep. If we lose it, castle day is still open. Prep and castle results line up only about 31% of the time.

  7. Confirm timing in-game

    The castle date (Sat 10 Oct) is estimated from the four-week cycle. Check the in-game KvK schedule and adjust the plan if it differs.

About the data

  • Activity counts come from a MightPulse snapshot taken 15 Sep at 10:48 UTC, three days after the last castle day. That is the newest activity data available for these alliances. Post-KvK activity tends to run high, so treat these as relative, not current.
  • MightPulse kingdom-wide figures (growth, Town Centre upgrades, active governors) were pulled 4 Oct 2026. Their freshness could not be confirmed.
  • StratForge's kingdom "real power" (2.8B / 2.7B) and "top-10 total" (8.3B / 8.4B) are smaller than single top alliances' real power, so the two metrics measure something narrower. They are compared like-for-like only.
  • K203's city and alliance counts come from a snapshot 17 days old.

  • Projections are judgement estimates, not model output.
