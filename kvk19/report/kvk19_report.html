Against us

  • K365 gained 4.4B of power in the last 7 days against our 2.9B (+53%). Prep points come from exactly this kind of growth: building, training and research.
  • K365 had 179 Town Centre upgrades in 7 days to our 117, and more governors active over 30 days (1,418 vs 1,060).
  • Prep is a whole-kingdom effort. K365 has 1,371 governors on the map to our 1,191.

For us

  • We lead Mystic Trial at every depth of the top 100: +0.7% in ranks 1–10, +3.5% in 11–50 and +3.8% in 51–100. Our hero strength is deeper, not just stronger at the top.
  • Our top 100 carry 12% more research power, a sign of more sustained investment.
  • We have never lost a prep phase (16-0). K365's only loss (14-1) was its first KvK.
  • Our top 6 alliances were at least as active as theirs in the 15 Sep snapshot (97% vs 95% active within 7 days).

Net: K365's growth and wider player base outweigh our hero depth, but only just. The growth figures could not be confirmed as current Unconfirmed; if they are stale, prep is closer to even.

Prep plan for K203

  1. Bank speedups and resources for prep

    K365 added 4.4B of power in the last 7 days to our 2.9B. We can't match that growth before prep, so make it count during prep: hold speedups, resources and upgrades until the matching prep day opens.

  2. Get the whole kingdom scoring

    K365 has 180 more governors on the map and 358 more active over 30 days. Prep points come from every account. Reach beyond the top 6 alliances: share the daily targets with every alliance in K203, not only the leading ones.

  3. Use our hero depth

    Our Mystic Trial lead is largest in ranks 51–100 (+3.8%). These mid-tier players are where we beat K365 head to head. Make sure each of them knows the daily targets and hits them, especially on hero-development days.

  4. Time Town Centre and building completions for prep

    K365 logged 179 Town Centre upgrades in 7 days to our 117. Upgrades that finish before prep score nothing in it. Queue long builds so they complete inside the prep window.

  5. Check standings every day

    Post the day's score gap in alliance chat each reset and shift effort to the next day's theme. A close day is decided in its final hours.

  6. Keep castle day separate

    If prep goes to K365, castle day is still open. Prep and castle results line up only about 31% of the time.

  7. Confirm the schedule in-game

    Castle day (Sat 10 Oct) and the prep days before it are estimated from the four-week cycle. Check the in-game KvK schedule.

About the data

  • Activity counts come from a MightPulse snapshot taken 15 Sep at 10:48 UTC, three days after the last castle day. That is the newest activity data available for these alliances. Post-KvK activity tends to run high, so treat these as relative, not current.
  • MightPulse kingdom-wide figures (growth, Town Centre upgrades, active governors) were pulled 4 Oct 2026. Their freshness could not be confirmed.
  • StratForge's kingdom "real power" (2.8B / 2.7B) and "top-10 total" (8.3B / 8.4B) are smaller than single top alliances' real power, so the two metrics measure something narrower. They are compared like-for-like only.
  • K203's city and alliance counts come from a snapshot 17 days old.
  • The castle date is an estimate from the four-week cycle (23 May, 20 Jun, 18 Jul, 15 Aug, 12 Sep).
  • Mystic Trial, research, hero, pet and troop figures are sums over each kingdom's top 100 leaderboard, captured 4 Oct.
  • Projections are judgement estimates, not model output.
